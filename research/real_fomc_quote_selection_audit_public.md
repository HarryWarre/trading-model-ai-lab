# Research 046 — FOMC quote-coverage audit (public summary)

Preregistered in [issue #63](https://github.com/HarryWarre/trading-model-ai-lab/issues/63) before computing the new coverage metrics. This work tests whether the previously reported Research 045 statement-time jumps can be generalized to the entire 2023–2025 period. It does **not** add a trading rule or backtest. [HistData's file specification](https://www.histdata.com/f-a-q/data-files-detailed-specification/) documents fixed EST source timestamps; official event dates are in the [Federal Reserve archive](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm). The announcement-time importance is motivated by [Acosta et al.](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2025-30.pdf).

For each of the frozen 24 FOMC events and 12 session-comparable instruments, require actual quotes at both five minutes before and five minutes after the official release. Compare availability with four earlier non-event days at the same New York clock time and weekday. Nearest quotes are diagnostics only, never substituted. Frozen broad-year pass rules: at least 23/24 events with eight assets in all three families and event/control quote availability within five percentage points in each family.

Result: **20/24** events pass; four consecutive 2023 event windows have no usable paired quotes, failing the broad-year completeness rule. Their earlier same-clock control windows also have near-zero coverage, consistent with a calendar-time data limitation rather than a uniquely FOMC-day gap. Overall family-level event/control availability gaps are 0.13 percentage points (FX), 1.04 (metals), and 1.56 (US indices). Only 50% of 2023 asset-event quote pairs are complete, versus 97.9% in 2024 and 100% in 2025.

The **observed** twenty-event mean from Research 045 was +10.465 basis points. Hypothetically setting four unobserved event differences to zero gives a twenty-four-event mean +8.721 basis points, and setting each to the smallest observed event difference gives +7.394 basis points. Neither assumption supplies missing data or resolves concentration of the observed mean in a few events. No P&L, four-pip fee result, probability of profit, or production claim follows.

Decision: Research 045 describes the 20 available events only. Its broad 2023–2025 generality **fails the frozen coverage gate**. Verify independent original-source quotes around the missing release windows before revisiting that claim; preserve an untouched later holdout. The audit runner and stdlib tests are in the repository. Input integrity is checked against existing manifests at execution, and private raw-file paths and detailed per-window provenance are intentionally not published in this summary.
