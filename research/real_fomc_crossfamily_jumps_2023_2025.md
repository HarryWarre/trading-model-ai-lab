# Research 045 — FOMC statement-time jumps across CFD families (ex-post mechanism, not trading)

## Frozen design and sources

Preregistered in [issue #62](https://github.com/HarryWarre/trading-model-ai-lab/issues/62) **before inspecting Research 045 outcomes**. [Acosta et al. (2025), Financial Market Effects of FOMC Communication](https://www.frbsf.org/wp-content/uploads/wp2025-30.pdf) distinguishes monetary-policy announcement and press-conference news; [Andersen et al. (2003)](https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/000282803321455151) documents high-frequency responses to macroeconomic announcements. Hypothesis: US statement times concentrate *unsigned* repricing across FX, metals and US equity-index references relative to past same-weekday, same-clock windows. This only tests an event risk mechanism, not forecast direction or tradable returns.

Input: the fixed-EST HistData 2023–2025 M1-to-right-closed/right-labeled 5-minute 15-asset panel (SHA-256 `6b5b91b058ec78c49b95ec48dde207157f18f7dbc4451e0bd1f15b4933a44942`), original raw-ZIP hashes in the panel manifest (manifest SHA-256 `cabb1aad9a8533121d123f762fac19454a41e744ba254c9a1c8a9d0a84a51db7`), and 24 release timestamps linked to [official Federal Reserve statements](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm) (event CSV SHA-256 `2f75e6409e1253cd22b3c9f507b8a6f1f5145668d65936627d76998aa14fd27f`). Analysis subset fixed at 8 FX, 2 metals and 2 US indices; two European equity indices closed at the US release and BCOUSD had incompatible coverage/session. No interpolated quotes. Local NY 14:00 is timezone-aware through DST.

For asset i, event e at T in UTC, define J(i,e)=10,000 |ln[P_i(T+5 min)/P_i(T−5 min)]| basis points. This is **not** an executable order: the right-labeled 14:00 bar may itself include an announcement quote. Choose the first four observed same-weekday 14:00 local windows from the prior 12 weeks, excluding FOMC dates, with exact before and after quotes. Let C(i,e) be their mean jump. Average J−C first equally within each family and then equally across the three families to obtain D(e). Do not backfill missing events from later dates. Use 10,000 circular 2-event-block bootstrap resamples of D, seed 20260916. Eligibility: >=8 assets, all three families, >=20/24 events; the frozen gates require D mean>0, bootstrap resample share of positive mean >=95%, >=2/3 years positive, every family positive and leave-one-family-out positive. The bootstrap share is **not a posterior probability** that a trading strategy makes money.

## Frozen-gate result

20/24 eligible events, 238 included asset-event observations. Event jump mean 16.9832 bp versus controls 6.5182 bp, event-weighted mean D **+10.4650 bp**; two-event-block resample share positive **99.15%**, percentile interval **[1.6305, 20.5689] bp**. All six preregistered gates passed *on observed eligible events*. By year: 2023 4 events +18.8871 bp; 2024 8 +12.7164; 2025 8 +4.0026. FX +8.3505, metals +16.4901, US indices +6.5544 bp; each family leave-one-out mean positive. See immutable parameters and input hashes in `results/research045/research045_manifest.json`.

## Crucial post-result diagnostics (not alternative pass gates)

Only **9/20** event D values were positive and median D was **−0.7367 bp**. The three largest positive events account for **87.63%** of the total *net* sum of D; omitting them reduces mean D to +1.5226 bp and the same bootstrap share to **79.62%**. This is an outlier-sensitive mean, not a reliably recurring jump at each meeting. Four consecutive 2023 meetings (March 22, May 3, June 14, July 26) have zero eligible assets; inspection of exact boundary prices finds gaps in the mixed-source panel at event times. Event exclusion is **not random with respect to macro-news dates** and can bias the mean. November 7, 2024 has 10/12 assets. The control audit records candidates labeled `selected` even when the subsequent event quote fails; the *included* control ledger has exactly four per included asset-event (952 rows). No after-the-fact cutoff, asset selection, or redating has been applied.

Decision: **mechanism supported only on the observed subset; research-only, not alpha**. No trade was simulated, so a four-pip CFD cost, turnover, 0x/1x/2x/4x stress, price-only trading baseline or capacity estimate would be meaningless. These become required if a genuinely ex-ante strategy is ever proposed. Current-year 2026 and live deployment remain untouched.

## Next research path and reproducibility

The [SF Fed US Monetary Policy Event-Study Database](https://www.frbsf.org/research-and-insights/data-and-indicators/us-monetary-policy-event-study-database/) (page reports an August 3, 2026 update) provides official event-study policy surprises differentiated by statement and press conference. `download_usmpd_official.py` validates raw XLSX magic, structure, member CRC, bounded size and hashes a snapshot; it does **not** imply that a source workbook was downloaded or that current-history shocks were available at trade time. This environment could not reach the workbook host. If a validated snapshot becomes accessible, audit event IDs, statement-versus-conference windows and release-time availability *before* joining with CFD returns; shock series built using revisions/full-sample principal components cannot silently be labeled point-in-time trade inputs. Investigate the four missing meetings using separately verified source archives before claiming broad-year generality, and obtain an untouched later-year holdout.
